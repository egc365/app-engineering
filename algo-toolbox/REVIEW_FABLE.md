# Technical review of toolbox_data.py and build_toolbox.py

Reviewer role: Six Sigma Master Black Belt and statistician. Scope: every formula, constant, threshold, mapping, roadmap branch, the project loop, and the Failure Pareto workbook formulas. Standards used: AIAG MSA 4th ed., AIAG-VDA FMEA 2019, standard SPC constant tables (d2, d3, c4 derived), Moore, McCabe and Craig IPS, Wing 2006 and standard algorithm design. Numeric claims were recomputed with /tmp/claude-0/review-fable/check.py. The Failure Pareto formulas were exercised by building the workbook into a scratch folder, injecting four test logs (seed rows, empty, ties, bad category text), recalculating with LibreOffice headless, and reading the results back (/tmp/claude-0/review-fable/pareto_test.py). No repository file other than this review was changed and nothing was committed.

## Verdict

**Ready with fixes.** 8 errors, 31 suggestions.

Every numeric constant and formula recomputes correctly: sigma level with the 1.5 shift (3.4 DPMO gives 6.00, 66807 gives 3.00, 308537 gives 2.00), I-MR constants (3/d2 = 2.659, D4 = 3.267 for n = 2), ndc = 1.41 (sqrt 2 = 1.414), both sample-size formulas, Cp and Cpk, Cohen's kappa, A2, D3, D4, A3, B3, B4 forms, p, np, c and u limits, and the IPS test formulas. The AIAG %GRR, ndc, kappa and Cpk bands match the cited standards. The Failure Pareto formulas are correct for ties and for an empty log.

The errors are concentrated in two places: the Western Electric rule statement is incomplete (it omits "consecutive" and "same side"), and the project loop has four termination holes (two cycles that bypass the term-limit check, an unbounded self loop at step 7, and a step 3 check that cannot distinguish a finished sub-problem from a finished project). The remaining errors are notation and labeling inconsistencies that would mislead a reader of the printed workbook.

## Items checked and found correct

- Sample size: n = (z* sigma / E)^2 and n = z*^2 p(1 - p) / E^2 (IPS writes m for E and uses p* = 0.5 as the conservative guess). Recomputed: sigma 10, E 2, 95% gives 96.04 -> 97; p 0.5, E 0.03 gives 1067.1 -> 1068.
- %GRR = sigma_GRR / sigma_total x 100 and the 10 / 30 bands, ndc = 1.41 PV / GRR with ndc >= 5 (AIAG MSA 4th ed.).
- Cohen's kappa formula, Fleiss for 3+ appraisers, and the 0.75 / 0.40 bands (AIAG MSA 4th ed. uses exactly these).
- Cp, Cpk, Pp, Ppk definitions and the 1.33 / 1.00 bands.
- DPMO formula and sigma level with 1.5 shift (recomputed at six DPMO values).
- Pareto cumulative percent, Fishbone 6M, 5 Whys stop rule, half-split steps = ceil(log2 N).
- 2^k effect definition and run count, steepest ascent description, Pugh scoring.
- Xbar-R limits (A2, D3, D4), I-MR constants 2.66 and 3.267 (3/d2 = 2.659, 1 + 3 d3/d2 = 3.267 at n = 2), Xbar-S (A3, B3, B4), np, p, c, u limits, subgroup bands 1 / 2 to 9 / 10 or more.
- IPS formulas: s, 1.5 IQR rule, z, SD(xbar) = sigma / sqrt(n), one-sample and paired t with df = n - 1, Welch t with software or conservative df, proportion CI, chi-square statistic, expected counts and df, b1 = r sy / sx, slope t with df = n - 2, F = MSG / MSE, two-way F tests.
- ANOVA equal-SD rule of thumb: "largest / smallest s < 2" matches IPS wording ("largest standard deviation less than twice the smallest").
- Power rule 0.80 and the "non-significant result proves nothing when underpowered" statement.
- Bootstrap: B >= 1000 and percentile CI match IPS chapter 16 practice.
- TEST_ROADMAP rows present are all correct pairings (one-sample t / signed rank, paired t / signed rank, Welch t / rank sum, ANOVA / Kruskal-Wallis, regression / Spearman, one-proportion z / exact binomial, two-proportion z / Fisher, chi-square / Fisher).
- CT definitions are standard (Wing 2006 pillars plus algorithm-design methods) and every remaining ct and st tag is defensible. Notable good mappings: CT-INV for control limits, CT-BFS / CT-DFS for fishbone vs 5 Whys, CT-GREEDY with the DOE caveat, CT-HEUR naming the thresholds as heuristics.
- PROJECT_LOOP steps 1, 2, 5 and the 3 -> 4 -> 5 -> 3 cycle terminate once the fixes in items 2 to 5 are applied, because every return to 3 then passes the term-limit check.
- build_toolbox.py: RANK on the tiebroken key yields unique ranks 1..n, INDEX/MATCH on literal ranks fills the sorted columns, the IF guard prevents #DIV/0! on an empty log, the integrity asserts catch dangling ct and st ids, openpyxl writes fullCalcOnLoad so Excel recalculates on open, and the secondary-axis line chart pattern is the documented openpyxl idiom.

## IPS chapter-number confidence

Confident (stable across IPS 6th, 7th, 8th and 9th editions, 2009 to 2017):

- Ch 1 Looking at Data: Distributions (descriptive statistics, 1.5 IQR rule, density curves and Normal distributions). ST-DESC, ST-NORM.
- Ch 2 Looking at Data: Relationships (correlation, least-squares line b1 = r sy / sx). The regression formula in ST-REG comes from here; the inference comes from ch 10. "1-2" for ST-DESC is acceptable, strictly ch 1.
- Ch 3 Producing Data (sampling design) and ch 5 Sampling Distributions (CLT). ST-SAMP "3, 5" is right; the CLT section is 5.2 in the 6th and 7th, 5.1 to 5.2 in the 8th and 9th.
- Ch 6 Introduction to Inference, section 6.4 Power and Inference as a Decision. ST-POWER.
- Ch 7 Inference for Means (7.1 one-sample and paired t, 7.2 two-sample t, 7.3 optional topics including the F test for spread). ST-T1, ST-TP, ST-T2.
- Ch 8 Inference for Proportions. ST-PROP.
- Ch 9 Inference for Two-Way Tables (titled Analysis of Two-Way Tables in older editions; goodness of fit is a later section whose number moved between editions). ST-CHI.
- Ch 10 Inference for Regression. ST-REG.
- Ch 11 Multiple Regression. ST-MREG.
- Ch 12 One-Way Analysis of Variance. ST-ANOVA1.
- Ch 13 Two-Way Analysis of Variance. ST-ANOVA2.
- Companion chapters, online only, same editions: 14 Logistic Regression, 15 Nonparametric Tests, 16 Bootstrap Methods and Permutation Tests, 17 Statistics for Quality: Control and Capability. ST-NP, ST-BOOT, ST-SPC, ST-CAP. Label all four "(companion)".

Uncertain:

- 10th edition (2021): I believe the numbering is unchanged, but I have not verified the table of contents. Treat ch 14 to 17 placement as the most likely thing to have moved.
- 5th edition (2006) and earlier: chapter titles match but section numbers differ, and the companion chapters were supplied on CD or online. Do not rely on the section-level notes above for those editions.
- Within-chapter thresholds moved between editions: the large-sample proportion CI condition was "at least 15 successes and failures" in the 6th edition and "at least 10" from the 7th. The workbook's 10 matches the recent editions.
- The 1.5 shift, Cpk bands, %GRR bands and kappa bands are not from IPS; chapter 17 covers control charts and capability indexes but not the Six Sigma conventions, so do not cite IPS for those.
